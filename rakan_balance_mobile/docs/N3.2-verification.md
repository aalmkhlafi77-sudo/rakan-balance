## N2 specific invariants
- A due record does not affect the ledger until payment.
- Partial and full payment each create one ledger transaction and matching allocation.
- Payment cannot exceed remaining amount or mismatch the due account, direction or allocation.
- Duplicate allocation and reminder IDs, orphan allocation/reminder and over-allocated payments are checked.
- Transfer pair validation now runs during mobile backup verification.
